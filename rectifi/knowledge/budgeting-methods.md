# Budgeting methods

A budget is a plan for your money before you spend it. It is not a punishment. Pick a method you will actually follow.

## The 50/30/20 split
Split what you take home into three parts: about half for needs (rent, food, transport, bills), about thirty percent for wants (eating out, games, trips) and about twenty percent for saving. Treat it as a starting point. A student on a small allowance may save less, and that is fine. Saving something every month matters more than hitting the exact split.

## Zero-based budgeting
Give every rupee a job at the start of the month until nothing is left unassigned. Income minus planned spending and saving should equal zero. It takes more effort, but you see where everything goes.

## Envelope or category limits
Set a monthly limit for each category, such as food or shopping. When a category is full, you stop spending there. In Rectifi this is the Budgets page: set a limit per category and watch the bar fill.

## Pay yourself first
Move your saving out on the day money arrives, before you can spend it. What is left is yours to spend without guilt.

## Keeping it going
Review once a week, not once a year. Small, frequent checks catch problems early. If you keep going over a limit, the limit may be unrealistic. Change it instead of giving up.
