# Rent agreements

A rent agreement is a written contract between you (the tenant) and the owner (the landlord). It protects both sides. A verbal promise is hard to prove.

## What it should say
- Names and addresses of the owner and the tenant, and the address of the property.
- The monthly rent, the due date and how it is paid. Prefer bank transfer or UPI so there is a record.
- The **security deposit** and when and how it will be returned.
- The start date, the length of the agreement and how much notice either side must give.
- Rules on rent increases, repairs, who pays for electricity, water and maintenance, and whether subletting is allowed.

## Registration and stamp duty
Rules for stamping and registering a rent agreement differ by state. In many places an agreement above a certain length must be registered. A stamped, registered agreement is stronger as proof, for example for an address proof or in a dispute. Ask a local professional or the state's official website.

## Before you sign
Read every page. Visit the place. Take photos of the condition of the flat on the day you move in and share them with the owner in writing. Make sure the person signing really owns the property or is authorised.

## Paying and records
Pay rent on time and keep every receipt. A rent record can help later for a rent allowance or tax claim. Log rent in Rectifi under the Rent category.

## When you leave
Give written notice as the agreement says. Do a joint walk-through. Ask for the deposit back in writing, with any deductions explained.
