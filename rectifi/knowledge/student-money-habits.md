# Student money habits

Most students have a small, fixed income: an allowance, a stipend or a part-time job. Good habits matter more than the size of the income.

## Know your numbers
Write down what comes in each month and what you must pay for: fees, rent or mess, travel. What is left is your free money. Log every payment, even small ones. Small payments add up, and the total is often a surprise.

## Simple habits that work
- Check your balance and your spending once a week.
- Set a limit for food delivery, shopping and subscriptions.
- Wait a day before buying anything you did not plan to buy.
- Split shared costs the same day. Do not let small debts between friends pile up.
- Keep some money for the end of the month, when allowances run low.

## Saving on a small income
Save a little right when money arrives, even a small amount. Use student discounts and shared plans. Compare before you buy. Cook some meals instead of ordering every day.

## Avoid
Buy now pay later schemes for things you want but do not need, borrowing from friends to cover regular spending and lending your UPI or bank account to others.

## Think ahead
Start an emergency fund early. Learn how a SIP works. Keep records of scholarships and stipends, because you may need them for tax forms later.
