# UPI safety and fraud

UPI is fast and safe when you follow a few rules. Almost every UPI scam works by tricking you into approving a payment yourself.

## The golden rules
- You never need your UPI PIN to receive money. Enter your PIN only when you are sending money.
- No real bank, shop or delivery person will ask for your PIN, OTP, card number or CVV. Never share them, even if the caller sounds official.
- A QR code is for paying. Scanning a QR code will never make someone send you money.

## Common scams
- **Collect request scam:** a stranger sends a request that looks like a refund or prize. Approving it takes money out of your account. Read every request before you approve.
- **Wrong number or refund calls:** someone says they sent money by mistake and asks you to send it back, or asks you to install a screen-sharing app. Do not install it.
- **Fake support numbers:** search results can show scam helpline numbers. Use the number printed on your card or the bank's official app.
- **Fake payment screenshots:** a seller may show a screenshot of a payment. Check your own bank balance or SMS before you hand over goods.

## If something goes wrong
Call your bank at once to block the account or card. Report the fraud on the national cyber crime helpline 1930 and at cybercrime.gov.in as soon as you can. The faster you report, the better the chance of freezing the money. Keep screenshots and the transaction reference.

## Good habits
Keep a small amount in the account linked to UPI, use a screen lock, keep the app updated and check the payee name shown before you pay.
