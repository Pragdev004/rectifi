# Managing subscriptions

Subscriptions are small payments that repeat every month or year: video and music apps, cloud storage, games, gym memberships. Each is small. Together they can be a large part of a student's budget.

## Why they slip by
They renew on their own. Free trials turn into paid plans. You may forget you still have them. Annual plans hide a big yearly cost in one charge.

## Find them
Look at your bank and UPI history for payments that repeat to the same payee. Rectifi's Insights page flags payments that recur and shows what they cost over a year. Also check the subscriptions list in your phone's app store.

## Decide with a simple test
For each one ask: did I use it in the last month? Would I buy it again today at this price? If both answers are no, cancel it. You can always sign up again.

## Save money
- Share a family or friends plan when the terms allow it.
- Use student pricing where it exists.
- Switch to a yearly plan only if you are sure you will keep it.
- Cancel before a trial ends. Set a reminder the day you start it.
- Turn off auto-renew when you can.

## Make it a habit
Review your subscriptions once every few months. Put them in a Subscriptions budget category so you see the total each month.
