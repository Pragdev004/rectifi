# Emergency fund

An emergency fund is money set aside for surprises: a medical bill, a broken phone, a lost scholarship payment or a sudden trip home. It keeps you from borrowing or selling investments at a bad time.

## How much
A common rule is three to six months of your basic expenses (rent, food, transport, bills). A student living on an allowance can start smaller, such as one month of basic costs, and grow it. Count only needs, not wants.

## Where to keep it
Keep it safe and easy to reach: a savings account or a liquid fund you trust. It is not for earning a high return. Do not keep it in shares or anywhere the price can fall just when you need the money. Do not keep it only in cash at home either.

## How to build it
Pick a small amount you can move every month and set it up on the day your money arrives. Keep it in a separate account so you do not spend it by accident.

## What counts as an emergency
A real, unplanned, necessary cost. A sale, a new phone you want or a party is not an emergency. After you use the fund, refill it before you start other saving goals.
