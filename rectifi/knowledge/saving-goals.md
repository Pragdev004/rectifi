# Saving goals

A goal with a name and a date is easier to save for than "I should save more". It could be a laptop, a trip, a course or an emergency fund.

## Set the goal
1. Name it and decide what it costs.
2. Pick a date.
3. Divide the cost by the number of months left. That is your monthly amount.
4. If the monthly amount is too big, move the date or change the goal. Do not skip the plan.

## Automate it
Move the monthly amount to a separate account on the day money arrives. When saving is automatic you need less willpower. Keeping goal money apart from spending money makes it harder to spend by accident.

## Choose where to keep it
- Needed within a year: a savings account or a fixed deposit.
- Needed in many years: you might consider an SIP, knowing returns are not guaranteed.
Do not put short-term goals in risky investments.

## Stay on track
Check progress weekly. Celebrate small steps. If you miss a month, add a little to the next ones instead of quitting. Rectifi's weekly review lets you set one small commitment, such as fewer delivery orders, to free up money for your goal.

## Order of priorities
First a small emergency fund. Then pay off any expensive debt, such as a credit card balance. Then save for bigger goals.
